Bring in your SMS history

Do this once on a Windows PC to load years of Arab Bank messages into Ledger. Everything happens in this browser: your messages are not uploaded anywhere.

1Back up your iPhone to this PC

  1. Connect your iPhone with a cable and open the free Apple Devices app (or iTunes). Both are in the Microsoft Store.
  2. Choose Back up all of the data on your iPhone to this computer. Leave Encrypt local backup unticked. Encrypted backups can't be read here.
  3. Click Back Up Now and wait until it finishes.

2Find the messages file

Copy one of these into the File Explorer address bar and press Enter:

%USERPROFILE%\Apple\MobileSync\Backup

Apple Devices app or Microsoft Store iTunes

%APPDATA%\Apple Computer\MobileSync\Backup

iTunes downloaded from apple.com

Open the newest folder inside, then the folder 3d. The messages file is the one named:

3d0d7e5fb2ce288813306e4d4636395e047a3d28

Can't see it? Paste that name into the search box at the top-right of File Explorer while in the Backup folder.

3Choose it here

4Save and send to your phone

  1. Send the file to your iPhone: email it to yourself, save it to iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself on WhatsApp. Then on the phone, save it to Files.
  2. In Ledger on your phone: Settings → Import backup and pick ledger-history.json.
  3. Done. Transactions already in Ledger are skipped, so importing twice is harmless. Delete the file afterwards.

🔒 This page reads the backup file with code that runs only in your browser (Ledger's parser and the open-source sql.js library, both served from this site). Nothing you choose here leaves this PC.
